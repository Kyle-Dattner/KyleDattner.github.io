For this class project, my group was tasked with dissecting a machine, analyzing it through a fluid mechanics lens, and making an instructional video that teaches others how it works. We were assigned a coffee machine, which turned out to contain many applications of fluid mechanics.

My role was to apply the hydrostatic equation and Bernoulli's equation to the machine. I analyzed the pressure differences created in the pipes, calculated the theoretical pressure difference the heating element must provide, and used Bernoulli's equation to find the theoretical exit speed of the fluid.

The full video is available on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4f8EQuzu2Fw).
