This cork interfaces with the AgXRP, which is designed for classrooms. It is easy to 3D print and shows how pumps can water plants. Tubes run into the water bottle, and the cork is used to reduce evaporation.

The cork has a variable diameter so that it can fit bottles with different opening sizes. It snap fits into openings from 20 to 24 mm, which covers most common water and soda bottles.
