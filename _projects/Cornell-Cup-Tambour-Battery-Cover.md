This battery cover was designed for the robot C1CO. It opens to give access to the battery and electronics without having to be removed from the robot.

![Back view of the battery cover]({{ "/assets/images/battery-cover-back.jpg" | relative_url }})

*Back view of the battery cover. The front view in the closed position is shown at the top of the page.*

![Side view of the battery cover]({{ "/assets/images/battery-cover-side.jpg" | relative_url }})

*Side view of the cover. The grooves that hold the top and bottom panels are visible, along with the groove that the tambour panels slide in.*

![Front view of the tambour panels]({{ "/assets/images/battery-cover-tambour-panels.jpg" | relative_url }})

*Front view of the tambour panels. In application, they are glued to a thin sheet that allows them to rotate relative to one another but not translate.*

![Close up of the snap fit mechanism]({{ "/assets/images/battery-cover-snap-fit.jpg" | relative_url }})

*Close up of the snap fit mechanism that lets the panels snap shut against the top of the cover. The handle piece also stops the panels from sliding down and out of the groove they ride in.*
